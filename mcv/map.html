About this map

This original schematic follows the station arrangement in the supplied reference: Salford Central to the left, Victoria platforms 6 to 1 from top to bottom, the turnback siding and Ordsall Chord below. It is not a scale or verified signalling diagram. Only Victoria platform associations are currently mapped; approach lines are context. Arrival reporting offsets are retained as evidence, not used as countdowns. Shared berth associations remain explicit. Descriptions expire after ten minutes without an update, so an empty lane does not prove that a platform is clear.

Snapshots publish approximately every 65 seconds; distribution can add delay. Stale snapshots hide train descriptions. The existing Traksy map remains available above.
