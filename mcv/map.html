About this map

This is an original schematic, not a scale signalling diagram. Arrival reporting offsets are retained as evidence, not used as countdowns. Shared berth associations remain explicit. Descriptions expire after ten minutes without an update, so an empty lane does not prove that a platform is clear.

Snapshots publish approximately every 65 seconds; distribution can add delay. Stale snapshots hide train descriptions. The existing Traksy map remains available above.
