Manchester Victoria at a glance

Published facility information, not a real-time accessibility assurance. Check the official page before advising a passenger.

MCV
Lift availability is not monitored here. Manchester Victoria is listed as step-free to all platforms, but individual lifts may be unavailable or have an unknown status. Station improvement works may also change routes through the station. Always check the current official station page and local station information.
